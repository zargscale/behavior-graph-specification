# Self-service sign-up

## Problem

Visitors email support to get an account, and wait a day for a reply.

## Who it is for

Visitors: people creating an account through the website. They see forms,
messages, and confirmation emails.

## Goals

- A visitor creates an account without contacting support.

## Rules

- A confirmation email never contains the visitor's password.

## Open

- Does an unconfirmed account expire?
